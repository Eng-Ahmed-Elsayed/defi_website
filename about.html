Digital Egypt For Investment Co. (DEFI) was incorporated pursuant to the Prime Minister's decision to solve a critical institutional need: bridging high-level national policy with resilient, enterprise-grade software execution across Egypt's ministries and public agencies.

Unlike traditional commercial consultancies, DEFI operates exclusively with a public-sector mandate. Every solution is architected according to Egyptian state data sovereignty regulations, Zero-Trust cybersecurity standards, and continuous inter-agency interoperability frameworks.
