ESTABLISHED AUTHORITY

Empowering Egypt's Public Sector Under Decree No. 132/2020

Digital Egypt For Investment Co. (DEFI) was founded pursuant to Prime Minister Decree No. 132/2020 to serve as the specialized technical and strategic arm supporting the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) and the Information Technology Industry Development Agency (ITIDA).

By combining enterprise-grade IT architecture with a deep understanding of governmental mandates, DEFI bridges the gap between ambitious national transformation policies and resilient, secure operational reality.

🏛️

Public Sector Focus

Dedicated exclusively to government authorities, public service agencies, and national projects.

🔒

Governance & Security

Rigorous compliance with Egyptian state data sovereignty, security, and quality benchmarks.

National Mandate & Mission

  • Modernizing Public Services: Transforming legacy administrative workflows into intuitive, citizen-centric digital platforms.
  • Interoperable Infrastructure: Establishing enterprise architecture frameworks that seamlessly link federal registries and services.
  • Operational Continuity: Managing and safeguarding mission-critical systems with 24/7 reliability and performance monitoring.
